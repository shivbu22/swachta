The process

Simple for you.
Powerful underneath.

Three taps to clean streets. A full logistics chain running behind it.

1
Post the job
Municipality officer, colony RWA, or citizen pins the location on the map, picks job type — sewer, garbage, sweep, lake — and posts. Instant or scheduled.
2
Worker accepts in seconds
The nearest trained Ozomin worker gets a push notification. They accept with one tap. You see their live location moving toward you on the map. ETA shown in real time.
3
OTP verification starts job
Worker arrives. You share a 4-digit OTP to confirm. Job begins. Worker takes a before photo for your proof of work record.
4
Waste logged & routed
Every kilo collected is photographed, categorised — dry, wet, metal, organic — and assigned a grade by AI camera. Worker navigates to nearest Ozomin warehouse.
5
Warehouse sells, worker earns
Warehouse staff weigh and verify the waste. Recyclables sold to buyers. Revenue flows back as worker bonus. You receive before/after proof and a warehouse receipt.

Why Ozomin

Not an app. An infrastructure.

Every other solution treats waste as a problem to ignore. Ozomin treats it as a resource to monetise — and workers as professionals, not tools.

01

Full-stack operator

We recruit, verify with Aadhaar, train, equip with PPE, and manage every worker. No freelancers. No uncertainty. The 15-minute arrival promise is real because we control every variable.

02

Hyperlocal clusters

Each city is geo-fenced into nano-clusters of 500m radius. Workers are stationed inside their cluster — never more than 300m from the next job. Distance is engineered out of the equation.

03

Waste becomes revenue

Snabbit's job ends when the cleaner leaves. Ozomin's job ends when the recycler pays. Every kilo collected is tracked, graded, sold, and turned into worker bonuses. Waste funds the operation.

04

Kavach safety system

AI-powered monitoring detects unusual noise or 15-min stillness during a job and auto-alerts the field officer. Hardware SOS fires even on a locked screen. Buddy pairing means no worker is ever alone.

Offline-first technology

Sewer workers lose signal underground. Lake pickers lose signal in remote areas. Ozomin's app works fully offline — jobs, checklists, waste logs, photos — all synced when signal returns.

Common questions

FAQs

How is Ozomin different from calling the municipality?
+
The municipality has no real-time tracking, no arrival guarantee, no proof of work, and no recycling chain. You make a complaint and wait days. Ozomin gives you a trained worker at your pinned location in 15 minutes, with before/after photos, a warehouse receipt proving where the waste went, and live GPS tracking throughout. The worker is accountable by name, rating, and Aadhaar — not anonymous.
Who can book a service on Ozomin?
+
Any municipality officer, RWA secretary, private company, NGO, or individual citizen can post a job on Ozomin. You log in with your phone number via OTP — no lengthy registration. Municipalities and companies get a dedicated admin dashboard for bulk job posting, worker tracking, and monthly compliance reports. Citizens use the simple 3-tap booking flow.
What happens to the waste after collection?
+
Every kilo collected is logged with photo, weight, and GPS coordinates. The worker navigates to the nearest Ozomin warehouse — OZ-01, OZ-02, or OZ-03 — where warehouse staff weigh and verify the delivery. Dry recyclable waste is graded A, B, or C and sold to verified recyclers. Wet organic waste goes to compost partners. Hazardous or non-recyclable waste is disposed through certified channels. You receive a full chain-of-custody receipt.
How are workers kept safe during sewer or lake jobs?
+
Three layers protect every Ozomin worker. The Kavach AI system monitors for unusual sound or 15 minutes of device stillness and auto-alerts the nearest field officer. The hardware SOS trigger fires by pressing the volume button 3 times — it works even on a locked screen inside a dark sewer. The buddy system pairs every worker with a nearby colleague who gets alerted if their partner goes silent. Every worker also has a live-location sharing button that they can activate to share their exact position with a trusted contact at any moment.
How does the 15-minute arrival actually work?
+
Ozomin divides every city into hyperlocal clusters of roughly 500-metre radius. Trained workers are stationed inside their assigned cluster throughout their shift — they are never travelling across the city. When a job is posted, the system assigns the nearest available worker who is already standing within 300 metres of the job site. As density increases and more workers join each cluster, arrival time decreases further. This is the same model Snabbit proved for home cleaning — Ozomin applies it to sanitation infrastructure.
Is Ozomin aligned with Swachh Bharat Mission?
+
Yes — by design. The Admin Dashboard auto-generates weekly and monthly reports in the format required by the Swachh Bharat Mission and Solid Waste Management Rules 2016. Every job has GPS-stamped before/after photo proof. The workforce is Aadhaar-verified and formally employed with insurance and bank accounts. Ozomin helps municipalities meet compliance targets without manual data collection.
How do workers get paid?
+
Workers receive a guaranteed monthly base salary of ₹25,000 to ₹30,000 transferred directly to their bank account. On top of that, they earn daily waste bonuses calculated per kilogram of recyclable material they deliver to the warehouse — Grade A plastic earns ₹1.50 per kg, aluminium ₹15 per kg. They can withdraw earned bonus money via UPI any time of day without waiting for the monthly cycle. Early login bonuses, streak bonuses, and referral payments are credited the same day.
