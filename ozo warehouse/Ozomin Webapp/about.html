Our story

We didn't build an app.
We built infrastructure.

Ozomin is India's first full-stack on-demand sanitation platform — connecting trained workers, real-time logistics, warehouse-grade segregation, and recycler payouts into one seamless chain.

The problem

India's ₹1.5 lakh crore sanitation gap is not a technology problem. It's an infrastructure problem.

Every Indian city has sanitation workers. What they don't have is a system that dispatches the right worker to the right location in under 15 minutes, tracks every kilo of waste collected, segregates it at warehouse level, sells recyclables at market rate, and credits the worker's UPI the same day.

Municipal systems rely on complaint registers. Private contractors hire workers with zero training, zero safety gear, and zero accountability. The waste they collect goes to the nearest dump — no segregation, no recycling revenue, no proof of work.

Ozomin was built to replace this broken chain with a complete operating system for urban sanitation — from the first job post to the last recycler invoice.

What we believe

Six principles we won't compromise

Every decision at Ozomin is filtered through these. They're not aspirational — they're operational constraints.

⏱️

15-minute promise

If a worker can't reach you in 15 minutes, our hyperlocal cluster model is broken. We fix the cluster, not the promise.

⚖️

Every kilo tracked

From street to warehouse to recycler — every gram of waste is photographed, weighed, graded, and accounted for. Zero dark matter.

💰

Waste pays for itself

We don't treat waste as a cost centre. Recyclable revenue funds worker bonuses, warehouse ops, and city expansion. The model sustains itself.

🛡️

Worker dignity first

Guaranteed salary, health insurance, PPE, Aadhaar-linked bank accounts, career progression. Workers are professionals, not gig temps.

📡

Offline-first tech

Sewer workers lose signal underground. Lake workers lose signal in remote belts. Our app works fully offline and syncs when signal returns.

🏛️

Government-grade compliance

Auto-generated SBM reports, GPS-stamped proof-of-work photos, Aadhaar-verified workforce. Built to pass any regulatory audit from day one.

Our journey

From one drain to an entire city

Ozomin's growth story — city by city, warehouse by warehouse.

1
Q1 2025

The idea — after watching a failed municipal cleanup

A 3-hour sewer blockage in Ghaziabad went unresolved because there was no system to dispatch a trained worker. Ozomin was conceived that week as a hyperlocal dispatch system for sanitation.

2
Q2 2025

First 20 workers trained and deployed in Sector 12

We recruited workers from the local community, verified them via Aadhaar, provided PPE and training, and deployed them in a single 500m cluster. First job accepted within 4 minutes.

3
Q3 2025

OZ-01 Warehouse goes live — waste becomes revenue

Our first warehouse in Loni started receiving worker-collected waste, segregating by material type, grading quality A/B/C, and connecting to verified recyclers. Workers earned their first per-kg bonuses.

4
Q1 2026

Ghaziabad-wide rollout — 3 warehouses, 100+ workers

OZ-02 and OZ-03 warehouses opened. The full 4-app ecosystem (Worker, Request, Warehouse, Admin) went live. Municipality partnership signed for automated SBM compliance reports.

5
Q2 2026 — NOW

Multi-city expansion begins

Noida and Meerut clusters under deployment. Kavach safety AI integrated. Lake & pond cleanup module live. Targeting 500 workers across 3 cities by end of year.
