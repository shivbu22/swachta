Quick answers

Common questions

How quickly will someone respond to my inquiry?
+
We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours. WhatsApp messages typically get a response within 1 hour during business hours. Partnership inquiries from municipalities receive priority and are handled within the same business day.
I want to book a service right now. What's the fastest way?
+
The fastest way is to call or WhatsApp us directly at +91 98765 43210. For instant booking, download the Ozomin Request App from the Play Store. You can have a worker at your location in under 15 minutes.
I represent a municipality. Who should I speak with?
+
Please use the partnership inquiry form on our Partner page or email partnerships@ozomin.in directly. Our deployment team will schedule a 30-minute discovery call and provide a custom proposal within 7 days.
Is Ozomin available in my city?
+
Currently live in Ghaziabad, UP. Noida and Meerut are in active deployment. If you're in another city, fill out the contact form and we'll add you to our expansion waitlist — we're growing fast and prioritise high-demand areas.
